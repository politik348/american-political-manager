# P1 R5 campaign

Prior verified state: 19 candidate WebPs committed, 23 expansion attempts, halted on Wikimedia rate limit. No candidate promoted to runtime. This campaign uses one worker, seven seconds minimum between uncached requests, persisted server pause, cache reuse, and ten serial chunks of at most 100 attempts. The first chunk must complete 100 attempts and add at least 60 distinct valid candidate images before other chunks can run; every chunk checks all candidate hashes, decodes and commits before the next begins. The threshold is an engineering decision, not an estimate of coverage. Any rate pause stops all remaining chunks; no quota bypass. A later run can resume after the recorded pause. Existing photos remain unchanged. Role matching for historical figures without life years requires exact stored Wikipedia title, name, and matching official office and overlapping Wikidata P39 term.
